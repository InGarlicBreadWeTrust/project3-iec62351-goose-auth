# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of experience, role, or affiliation — especially for the
rural co-operative and small-utility volunteers who this project exists to help.

## Our Standards

Promote a welcoming, open, and safety-conscious environment. Contributions to
safety-critical protection logic must be:
- Constructive and good-faith.
- Backed by conformance or test evidence, not speculation.
- Focused on the defensive, passive, authentication goals of this scaffold.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, hate speech, or conduct that
undermines the safety-first purpose of this work.

## Enforcement

Incidents may be reported to the maintainers (see `SECURITY.md`).
Maintainers act to address reports.

This project adheres to the [Contributor Covenant v2.1].

[Contributor Covenant v2.1]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
